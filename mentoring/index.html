Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. It's education and implementation support. Where something needs a solicitor, accountant, broker or regulated adviser, we say so and you should use one.

Will I make money in 90 days?

Nobody honest can promise that. The 90 days are measured in actions you control: registrations done, conversations had, deals properly evaluated.

Do I need money to start?

Not for every door. Referring needs very little. Owning needs the most. The path session matches the door to the money you could responsibly use, and we never suggest borrowing to start.

Is it one-to-one?

It's group mentoring for twelve people, with a one-to-one path session at the start. Private one-to-one mentoring is separate and selective.

What if it isn't right for me?

Then I'll say so on the call. Before you pay, you get the price, the instalments, cancellation rights and support limits in writing.
