FROM THE WORK, NOT JUST THE THEORY

“What am I actually buying?”

Martin has worked across single lets, refurbishments, HMOs and commercial conversions, including conversion projects worth £2.5m. His approach starts with asking better questions: is the building straight and true, what does the title reveal, is there knotweed, and what does a flood warning mean for this particular property?

Those questions sit alongside the numbers, demand and delivery plan. The aim is a buying decision you can explain — including the decision to leave it alone.
