Where are you starting from?

Eight quick questions. No right answers, no email needed. You get your level, the one free sheet to read first, and the module the course starts you on. Most people guess too high; the quiz doesn't.

1. Have you ever bought a property, in your own name or a company?
2. "Exchange", "completion", "LTV", "yield". How many of those could you explain to a mate without looking them up?
3. Have you ever let a property to a tenant?
4. Could you work out, on paper, whether a £100,000 terrace renting at £650 a month makes sense with a 75% mortgage?
5. Money you could put into property in the next six months without touching an emergency fund
6. Hours a week you can actually give this, most weeks
7. Have you ever spoken to an estate agent, a vendor or a landlord about a deal, rather than about a home to live in?
8. What do you want property to be for you?
