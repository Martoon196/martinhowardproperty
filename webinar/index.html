YOUR HOST

At 30 I was packing cheese in a factory in Somerset.

I'd already built a cleaning business from two people to nineteen staff, and a family crisis meant starting again from zero. Years earlier, at fifteen, I'd spent a summer cleaning my grandfather's engineering factory and noticed the building was earning more than the business inside it. The value isn't always in the obvious thing.

In 2017 I took a property course, tried serviced accommodation, found it didn't suit me, and moved to sourcing in Swansea. I also learned the hard way what a handshake deal costs, which is why everything I teach starts with paperwork. Today I work on property projects in South Wales and mentor a small number of people by application.
